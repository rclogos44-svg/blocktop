Left stick moves. Hold SHOOT and let go inside the green. Tap SPRINT to turn sprint on or off, then press SHOOT near the rim to dunk; without sprint you lay it up. PASS steals on defense (in MyCourt it gets you a ball). SPIN spins; double-flick the stick for crossovers, breakdowns and stepbacks. Two-player modes need a keyboard.

Jumpers are green or miss. Your shooting attribute sets the green size, and a defender's contest shrinks it. Strength decides who wins body contact. After a steal or defensive rebound, take it past the arc before you shoot.
